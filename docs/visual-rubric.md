# Silver-Gold manual visual regression rubric

Version: 0.1.0  
Scope: SGP#7  
Decision owner: manual reviewer

## Boundary

This rubric does not automate subjective visual quality. Static validators may verify file structure, declared ratios, checksums, coverage, and evidence links. They must not claim perceptual metal allocation, material realism, identity fidelity, anatomy, construction quality, composition fidelity, or target-size readability.

The SVG files in `assets/examples/` are deterministic synthetic QA anchors generated by repository code. They are not outputs from an image generator and are not presented as production artwork.

## Review surfaces

Every visual case requires two separate reviews:

1. **Full-size review** at the native 1024 × 1024 view.
2. **Target-size review** at 64 × 64 or the declared product size, whichever is smaller.

A full-size PASS does not imply target-size PASS.

## Review order

1. Confirm the expected outcome and selected background profile.
2. Confirm the primary subject, action, and semantic meaning.
3. Check identity and construction locks where applicable.
4. Compare declared silver/gold allocation with perceived visual weight.
5. Inspect material and reflection behaviour.
6. Inspect geometry, edges, folds, and silhouette.
7. Inspect controlled rim light, shadows, and profile-specific background behaviour.
8. Inspect exact text/logo workflow and deterministic overlay safe area.
9. Repeat checks at target size.
10. Record `accepted`, `rejected`, or `ambiguous` with evidence and reviewer notes.

## Accepted

Use `accepted` only when all critical gates pass:

- silver is the structural base and gold remains an accent;
- perceived allocation is consistent with the declared 70–85 / 15–30 contract;
- surfaces read as matte or satin rather than mirror, chrome, or jewelry;
- geometry is low-poly/faceted with clean edges and readable silhouette;
- rim light is controlled and shadows are clean;
- the selected background profile is respected;
- required identity, object, camera, composition, and text locks are preserved;
- the asset remains readable at target size;
- the final asset can be surfaced to the user.

## Rejected

Reject regardless of numeric score when any critical defect is visible:

- gold dominance or silver no longer reads as the base;
- glossy, mirror, chrome, liquid-metal, jewelry, or baroque treatment;
- black-gold / Obsidian-like contamination;
- noisy or dirty metallic texture;
- wrong background profile;
- lost identity, incorrect anatomy, or construction failure;
- unreadable silhouette at target size;
- damaged required text/logo or missing deterministic overlay step;
- final asset is not available to the user.

## Ambiguous

Use `ambiguous` when static contracts pass but perceptual judgement remains uncertain, for example:

- declared 70/30 allocation appears visually more gold-heavy;
- rim light exists but may be too weak at target size;
- silhouette is readable at full size but marginal at 64 px;
- composition fidelity is close but not clearly preserved.

Ambiguous cases require an owner decision. They are not silently promoted to accepted.

## Limitations

- SVG anchors demonstrate structural examples only; they do not prove generator reproducibility.
- Metal ratio is declared and then manually judged; no pixel-perfect CV score is a source of truth in v0.1.0.
- A generator or model upgrade invalidates prior visual evidence unless the exact asset bytes remain unchanged.
